# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

SoroSLO contributors are expected to collaborate professionally, respectfully, and constructively.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code on their technical merits.
- Give actionable review feedback.
- Assume good faith while still requiring evidence for technical claims.
- Respect privacy and security boundaries.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and participation that violate these expectations. Serious or repeated misconduct may result in removal from project spaces.

For conduct concerns that should not be public, contact the maintainers through a private channel listed by the repository owner.
